# The Fellas OS v9 — Full Trade & Draft Engines

## Trade Engine
- 55 Sleeper trade events found from 2020 through 2026 Week 6.
- 51 are reconstructed from draft ownership + chronological transaction history / verified record-book context.
- 4 historical events remain deliberately UNGRADABLE because player direction/details cannot be proven.
- Outcome metric = actual Fellas starter points delivered after the trade, beginning the following full fantasy week.
- Direct utility is separated from process, keeper value, later flips and legacy.

## Draft Engine
- 1,050 Sleeper draft selections audited, 2020–2026.
- Completed open-market picks use actual regular-season Fellas starter points delivered to the drafting manager.
- Utility is round-adjusted against the completed-era median for that round.
- Same-season traded picks are labeled TRADED rather than falsely graded as busts.
- Keepers are labeled KEEPER.
- Playoff starter points are displayed separately.
- 2026 grades are provisional pace grades through Week 6.

## Accuracy
This build intentionally preserves uncertainty rather than inventing missing history.
