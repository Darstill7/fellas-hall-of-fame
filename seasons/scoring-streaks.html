How the records are calculated

A game counts only if its actual matchup score is strictly greater than the threshold (100.00, 120.00 or 150.00 does not qualify). Consecutive means adjacent played fantasy weeks for the same manager. The all-games category includes recorded playoff and consolation weeks; regular-season records exclude them. Future games are never presumed to qualify.

Scoring-system differences across eras prevent direct comparisons of scoring difficulty. The verified source is Sleeper season archives 2020–2025 plus completed 2026 Weeks 1–8. ESPN 2016–2019 requires a separate weekly-score audit, particularly for two-week playoff aggregates. Archived 2023 roster-2 identity evidence requires reconciliation before awarding any streak to a manager.
