Largest measured post-trade advantage

Tom · 2021

Birch received Lamar Jackson, Mark Andrews, and Josh Jacobs; Thommy received Dak Prescott, Noah Fant, and Antonio Gibson. In subsequent regular-season starts, Birch's incoming group generated 263.54 position-relative points above baseline versus 131.75 for Tom. A major measured advantage, without blaming any later rule change.
