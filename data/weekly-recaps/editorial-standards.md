# Fellas Network editorial standards

Commissioner-approved November 24, 2026.

## The Tuesday Hangover
Tuesday publication after final matchup data is available. Tagline: Sunday's decisions. Tuesday's consequences. Recap all five matchups, roast owners, explain actual NFL performances, injuries and bye-week context, highlight weekly awards and records, summarize standings, streaks and playoff race. Trades may be covered for immediate matchup impact. Do not include a full upcoming-week preview, writer betting lines or prediction ledger.

## The Weekly Shitter
Normally Thursday, before the first NFL kickoff of the upcoming week; move earlier when games begin earlier (Thanksgiving week 2026: Wednesday November 25). Long-form features, trade strategy and grades, historical storylines, matchup previews, editorial spreads and totals, review of prior published predictions. Preserve the approved Issue 02 masthead, dark-mode editorial identity and fictional sponsorship disclosure.

## Shared standards
Separate branding, archives, issue numbering and share URLs. iMessage-first: original typographic JPEG preview, absolute Open Graph metadata, mobile Safari validation, no AI football equipment imagery. Verify real NFL reporting and final league data every edition; distinguish injuries, byes and zero fantasy points. Incorporate commissioner context without inventing quotes. Scott and Stover are brothers; Mitch and Kuhl are Colts fans. Preserve original predictions for accountability. No publication until relevant editorial, visual and data checks pass.
