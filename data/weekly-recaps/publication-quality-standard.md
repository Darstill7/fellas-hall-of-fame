# Fellas editorial release standard

Applies to The Tuesday Hangover and The Weekly Shitter. Adopted November 24, 2026 after Stew identified a published winning-streak error.

## Required evidence before publication

1. Freeze the completed-week cutoff. Reconcile all ten managers and all completed matchups against the canonical ledger and current Sleeper results. A recent-game window is not sufficient for streaks. Verify contiguous coverage, wins/losses, PF, streaks, ranking, margins, scoring awards and ties.
2. Check every named player score against the actual matchup lineup; distinguish starters, bench, zero points, bye, injury and inactive status. Verify trade dates, participants, receiving-team starts and historical championship claims against transaction and historical records.
3. Verify NFL scores, injury claims, bye weeks and kickoff times against dated reliable sources. Zero fantasy points alone never prove unavailability.
4. Recompute historical records with the correct regular-season/postseason scope and issue-specific cutoff. Verify every superlative against the entire eligible dataset.
5. Maintain a claim ledger for each issue: exact claim, source, cutoff, calculation or evidence, and verified/unverified status. Unsupported factual claims must be corrected, qualified or removed. No fabricated quotes or sources. Satire must not disguise inaccurate numerical claims.
6. Independently review the actual rendered article against the claim ledger, including title, deck, captions, awards, standings, charts, social image, metadata and homepage. Review prose separately from data tests. Do not claim a manual review occurred unless actually performed.
7. Tuesday Hangover: roast-first, all ten managers, completed matchups and season context. Weekly Shitter: features, trade strategy, previews and prior prediction receipts; generally Thursday, earlier before earlier NFL kickoff. Preserve each publication's identity. Commissioner context enhances jokes, not evidence.
8. Require successful applicable editorial, canonical, mobile and visual checks on the exact release commit. Verify live article, homepage and JPEG after deployment; distinguish browser emulation from native iMessage verification. Do not call an issue fully verified while checks are pending.
9. Corrections: fix all occurrences, retain original URL, add concise dated correction note, re-run applicable tests and verify deployment. Never quietly change a previously published prediction.

## Automation boundary
The Week 11 Hangover streak test enforces complete season coverage and selected aggregate claims. A reusable issue-level claim ledger and automated verification for player, trade and external NFL facts remain required engineering work; this policy alone does not implement those checks.
