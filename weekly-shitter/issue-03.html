MATCHUP 01 / The Folding Chair Fight

Stilly (7–4) vs. JMid (5–6)

Editorial spread / totalJMid -2.5 / 228.5
Columnist's scoreJMid 119–113

Cook, Lamb and Chase give Stilly the stars; Johnson, Hall and Pollard give JMid the backfield edge. The season scoring gap is just 1.35. Stilly's young FLEX options could flip this.

THE PICK · JMid -2.5 · Over 228.5

232 projected points; slim over.

Full matchup analysis

The number: Stilly averages 115.54 to JMid's 114.19. That's practically a coin flip before personnel. Cook, Lamb and Chase give Stilly the stars, but Gordon and two young FLEX options leave plenty of room for a Sunday afternoon identity crisis. JMid has Hall, Pollard and Johnson in the starting nine, plus Kittle. Johnson benefiting from Walker's absence is the reason to shade the line toward JMid.

What changes it: If Stilly's FLEX options produce or his elite receivers erupt together, the lean flips quickly. JMid needs his receivers to do more than stand next to the ball.

Total: A 232-point forecast is only a slim over lean. The two teams have enough offensive talent to reach it, but this is not a high-confidence total.

MATCHUP 02 / The Championship Reunion Tour

Tony (8–3) vs. Thommy (3–8)

Editorial spread / totalTony -8.5 / 225.5
Columnist's scoreTony 119–108

Tony has a 10.78-point season scoring edge and Taylor back. Thommy's Goff–receiver group can keep it interesting, particularly if Bowers sits.

THE PICK · Tony -8.5 · Over 225.5

227 projected; weakest over.

Full matchup analysis

The number: Tony averages 112.28 against Thommy's 101.50. Dak, Taylor, Javonte and the receivers give Tony the steadier starting lineup, but Bowers' ankle adds uncertainty. Thommy has Goff, Nabers, Higgins and Waddle, so there is an actual path to a competitive score. Whether the running backs cooperate is another matter entirely.

What changes it: Bowers sitting would reduce Tony's advantage. A strong Goff and receiver showing could keep Thommy within a touchdown of the spread.

Total: The forecast clears the total by only 1.5. This is the weakest over of the week, not a claim that Tony has suddenly become the 1999 Rams.

Stew (8–3) vs. Birch (7–4)

Editorial spread / totalBirch -2.5 / 244.5
Columnist's scoreBirch 126–120

Stew leads season scoring, but Birch has Mahomes, Henry, Amon-Ra and McBride. That's an obscene grocery bill. Gibbs and Coleman can erase the narrow edge.

THE PICK · Birch -2.5 · Over 244.5

245.21 combined season average; close to fair.

Full matchup analysis

The number: Stew averages 126.73; Birch 118.48. By season scoring Stew deserves respect, but Birch has Mahomes, Henry, Amon-Ra, Rice and McBride on the same starting roster. That's an obscenely expensive Thanksgiving grocery bill. Walker is reported out; Coleman and Gibbs keep Stew dangerous, and Collins is in the FLEX. I'm narrowly favoring Birch's available personnel, not betting against seven weeks of history for fun.

What changes it: A repeat of Coleman's Week 11 explosion or Gibbs going nuclear makes Birch's slight edge disappear. This is the matchup of the week for a reason.

Total: The combined season baseline is about 245.21. Both have a path to 120-plus; the total is close to fair.

Mitch (8–3) vs. Kuhl (0–11)

Editorial spread / totalMitch -36.5 / 210.5

214 projected; fragile over.

Total: The predicted total is 214. A returning Mitch lineup supports the over, but Kuhl's scoring floor makes this a fragile pick.
