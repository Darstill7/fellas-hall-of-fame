## Defensible records and context

- **Lowest starting seed to win a title:** Koble, **No. 6 in 2020**. The only No. 6 champion in the verified 2016–2025 record.
- **Champions from a No. 4-or-lower seed:** Thommy (2018, No. 4), Koble (2020, No. 6), Stilly (2023, No. 4). **Three of ten**, but the first two seasons used a four-team bracket and must not be used to suggest identical field probability.
- **No. 1 seed became champion:** Thommy (2017) and Birch (2019), **two of ten titles**. These are season facts, not an estimate of the long-run success rate under one unchanged playoff format.
- **First-round-bye champions:** Birch (2019), Tony (2021), Stover (2022), Thommy (2024), Scott (2025). **Five of eight seasons** with a six-team field (2018–2025). The other three were non-bye seeds.
- **Top seed denied the trophy:** eight of ten completed seasons. Do not interpret regular-season strength as irrelevant; postseasons are small samples.

## Eligibility and quality safeguards

- 2016 and 2017 have four championship-bracket participants and no bye; 2018 onward have six with two byes.
- Winner-bracket standings only. No losers-bracket advancement means a championship berth.
- The 2020 No. 6 upset is a verified *seed* record even if incomplete weekly player boxscore coverage prevents an invented run-value grade.
- Evans and other former managers retain their historical participation; do not rewrite their former slot as JMid in 2023.
- Do not attribute championships based solely on matchups, strength of schedule, draft performance, or would-have-won counterfactuals.

**Publication proposal:** Small, fact-focused `Road to the Ring` exhibit in Championship Stories or Records with winners-by-seed and a clearly labeled six-team bye count, not a 'playoff luck score'.
