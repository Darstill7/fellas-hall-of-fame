# Verified Fellas Postseason Records — Winners Bracket Only

**Research dated November 9, 2026.** Computed from official season weekly-score history intersected with verified ESPN/Sleeper championship brackets; raw evidence in `data/postseason_winners_bracket_record_receipts_2016_2025_v1.json`. **Research candidate, not yet a website award.**

## Source and coverage rules

- **41 recovered championship-bracket game receipts across 2016–2025**, including ten confirmed finals. These are **recovered** games, not proof that every historically played playoff game has a retained score. 2020 particularly has incomplete earlier-round score coverage.
- **2016 and 2017 ESPN results are two-week aggregate totals**, intentionally excluded from the *single-week* margin and points leaderboards below. Those two years belong in a separately labeled aggregate chart.
- **Sleeper `postseason_unclassified` entries are not automatically championship games.** Accept only matchups with verified official winner-bracket membership for that specific round and week. All other games, including consolation, are excluded.
- Historical managers remain historical: Evans is the 2023 seat owner, not JMid. Only managers active in 2026 appear in the manager-level shortlist, but bracket records themselves may involve former managers.
