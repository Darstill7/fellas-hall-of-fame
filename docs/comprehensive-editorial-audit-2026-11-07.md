# The Fellas: ten-championship and ten-manager editorial accuracy review

**Audit window:** completed seasons 2016–2025, and 2026 through verified Week 8. **Nature:** source-backed PR review, not a new final 2026 season result.

## Executive findings

- All ten champions, official championship opponents, winner seeds, regular-season records and archived title scorelines were independently reconstructed from the canonical weekly matchups, season guide and winner-bracket sources.
- The ten published championship articles contain four acts each and roughly 472–520 words; six Sleeper-era titles have verifiable semifinal and championship starter box scores. The four ESPN-era titles do **not** have an archival basis for individual game MVP claims.
- All ten current managers have substantive three-paragraph archival biographies with distinct career records and ownership windows. 2023 belongs to Evans; JMid joined in 2024.
- **Material editorial correction in this PR:** the 2022 championship previously described the final as an uncomplicated blowout. The recorded Stover 99.22–Stew 48.16 remains authoritative, but the canceled Buffalo–Cincinnati NFL Week 17 game makes an unqualified narrative misleading. The story now includes that limitation. NFL sources: [cancellation](https://www.nfl.com/_amp/week-17-buffalo-cincinnati-game-will-not-be-resumed-neutral-afc-championship-gam), [fantasy scoring impact](https://www.nfl.com/_amp/nfl-fantasy-football-bills-bengals-scoring-update). This is not a revision of official league standings or a claim about what would have happened otherwise.
- Five new story-worthy record discoveries are derived and locked to their source receipts. They are **candidate categories**, not injected into the public canonical Record Book.
