# Trade Museum publication format

User direction: concise, easily digestible website summaries backed by full investigative research. Target 100–150 words of visible narrative per trade, not a hard cap for exceptional multi-team deals. Use a specific headline, concise context and outcome, individual manager grades with one-sentence rationale once certified, and expandable supporting evidence. Keep transaction assets, dates and grade badges scannable. Manager profiles and homepage use shorter excerpts linking to the shared canonical trade entry. Preserve all dated NFL sources, ownership spells, roster snapshots, lineup comparisons, regular-season and verified paired playoff impact in research records. Distinguish verified facts from inferred strategy and avoid claiming injury explains a bench decision without evidence. Dry wit and occasional crude language are welcome when earned; no favoritism. Do not publish uncertified letter grades. Existing 60/30/5/5 weights unchanged; voided picks have zero realized value and any intent adjustment remains proposed. Mobile-first: collapsed evidence by default, accessible disclosure controls, no duplicate narratives or conflicting grades.
