## Other strong future record families, requiring additional computation or source work

1. **Biggest late-season playoff push:** minimum standing after Week 8 followed by largest improvement in playoff seed or ranking; require seed snapshots to distinguish estimated versus official standings.
2. **Most wins needed to save a season:** maximum consecutive wins after a losing record and before qualifying; derive from canonical weekly matches.
3. **Closest championship escape:** minimum verified title-game victory margin, reporting 2016–17 two-week aggregates separately from single-week finals.
4. **Biggest title-game upset:** championship winner's seed minus runner-up seed, with official seed pairing and round format.
5. **Hardest path to a ring:** count playoff opponents and their seeds / regular-season records; compare only compatible field formats.
6. **Fewest points for by a champion:** use regular-season points per game or within-season rank, not raw totals across unlike scoring systems.
7. **Most points scored without making playoffs:** complete-season fantasy points relative to own season field; exclude inconsistent partial samples.
8. **Best regular season without a championship:** best completed regular-season win percentage among non-champions; ties by wins and source coverage.
9. **Cruelest weekly loss:** highest losing team score in a head-to-head matchup, plus within-week all-play rank for contextual badge.
10. **Greatest comeback matchup:** season-by-season final score alone is insufficient to claim in-game comeback; requires archived live/projection trajectory. Do not publish as verified without play-by-play.
11. **Trade that launched a champion:** original receipts plus roster ownership and subsequent start/points; narrative, not an algorithmic trade winner.
12. **Biggest playoff heartbreaker:** narrowest verified elimination margin, with multiweek ESPN finals identified separately.
13. **Most repeated title-game opponents:** repeat matchups across seasons; count verified brackets only.
14. **Most years as a top-two seed without a title:** derive from verified bracket seeding and exclude seasons not in league.
15. **Longest playoff drought ended:** require complete season membership and bracket-qualified participation, including ownership handoffs.
