| Observed positional-median pattern | Count | Proper interpretation |
|---|---:|---|

The test is **not** the original qualified 18-deal PAR method. It subtracts each position/week's actual lineup-start median from each acquired player's subsequent started fantasy points; this yields descriptive results, not counterfactual transaction profit. Multi-player and zero-start packages may be heavily affected by lineup usage, number of starts, injuries, transfers, flex-slot opportunity costs, and acquisition date. A team that never started a player has observed starter return 0, but unknown bench, keeper or trade option value.

## Proposed 0–100 rating contract — *not scored*

- **50 means neutral expected benefit** from a trade relative to legal, contemporaneously available alternatives; zero and 100 are conceptual bounds, not automatically assigned to historic highs/lows.
- Compute **independent absolute benefit for each manager first**. A winner/loser margin is a **separate differential**; both managers may improve or both may be harmed.
- Model observed starter positional contribution, positional replacement opportunity, weeks remaining and roster lock, waiver/FAAB spending or receipts and their later use, keeper cost and rights, and playoff value. Avoid assigning zero penalties for unmeasured areas; mark those components **unassessed**.
- Use shrinkage/uncertainty proportional to comparable sample size and data coverage, so 1–2 historic trades cannot masquerade as proven long-term skill.
- Career manager labels are editorial analyses of transaction patterns, and should be held until independently supported; do not confuse a descriptive title with a verified score.
