## Corrections applied in this copyedit

1. **2021 — precise defensive contribution:** Tampa Bay's 19.00 fantasy points are now described as a starter contribution, not a fictional standalone 19-point winning margin.
2. **2022 — chronology:** The close Week 5 victory is now described as an **October** event, not September; the canceled Buffalo–Cincinnati Week 17 game caveat remains intact.
3. **2023 — cleaner opening:** Removed a second explanation of the same 0–4 start; retained exact opponents, scores, sources and trade consequences.
4. **2024 — timeline and six scorer proof:** Five full seasons intervened between Thommy's 2018 and 2024 titles (2019–2023). The title-game lineup had **six** scorers of at least 22, including Jameson Williams's 22.00; the archived text listed only five.
5. **2025 — no invented award:** Replaced title-game 'MVP partnership' wording with the observed two highest winning-starter scores.

## Coverage and unresolved evidence

- **2016–2019:** The available ESPN archive establishes matchup scores and brackets, not a full individual player starter ledger. An individual playoff MVP, starter contribution, transaction-level championship impact or regular-season WAR rating cannot be claimed.
- **2020–2025:** Semifinal and final winner-side starting lineups exist. Scores can support named performers but *cannot* establish subjective manager intent, missing bench production or what an alternative lineup would have done.
- **2021 and 2024:** The explicitly linked trade receipts are named evidence, not a complete league transaction inventory. For 2024, the archived trade audit identifies additional unlinked deals; a missing link does not mean no trade took place.
- **2022:** The reported champion is Stover under the official recorded result; no hypothetical reconstruction of the canceled Bills–Bengals NFL game is published.
- **2023:** Evans was the historical 2023 league participant; JMid joined afterward. Keep that separation in bios, opponents and editorial recaps.
