## Editorial guidance

- Call the highest scorer **championship-game top scorer** or **finals scoring leader**, not automatic season MVP. A championship MVP designation may be appropriate as *an editorial award* with a defined scoring criterion.
- Defensive/special-teams slots are retained if they appear among the top three; do not erase them to favor marquee players.
- Compare each final to the canonical bracket and keep season-specific format distinctions in older ESPN years.
- For trades, only say an acquired player affected the starting lineup if a trade record and credited original-manager starter score both exist. The 2024 Kelce acquisition is such an example.
- Do not substitute a player's best game in the semifinal or regular season for their actual final output.
