# Championship anthology — editorial fact-check and storytelling standard

**Scope:** The Fellas championship features, 2016–2025. Use manager nicknames only.

## Reporting rules

- Every championship owner, opponent, score, seed and eliminated manager must reconcile with `season_guide.json`, the verified ESPN/Sleeper playoff brackets and `season_weekly_matchups_2016_2026_v1.json`.
- The 2016–2017 championship finals are **two-week aggregate scores**, not one NFL week. The league's scoring rules evolved; avoid direct player-point comparisons across eras.
- A verified transaction does **not** prove it was the only cause of a win. Link trade effects to a documented starter appearance and exact points when that evidence exists.
- Individual title-week player MVP attribution is supported by archived starter box scores for 2020–2025 only. Do not fabricate ESPN-era fantasy player awards, strategic motives, waiver bids or unverified lineup alternatives.
- For 2022, acknowledge the canceled Buffalo–Cincinnati game and report the archived league championship result. Do not claim that an invented resimulation determines the rightful champion.
- Give each article a distinct arc: before the playoffs, the vulnerable moment, verified decisive players/opponents, and what that championship changed about league history.
- Avoid generic claims such as “destiny,” “best team ever,” or “greatest comeback” unless a precise record supports the superlative.
