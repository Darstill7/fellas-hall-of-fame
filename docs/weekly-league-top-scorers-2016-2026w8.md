## Qualifications

- **2016 had eight teams**, not ten; weekly rank comparisons have fewer potential opponents.
- **2020/21 Week 14 is incomplete** and is excluded from all managers' weekly tops, not guessed from available eight-team partial scores.
- **No weekly leader ties were found** in the recovered fully paired score weeks; the underlying data model nevertheless supports tied top scorers.
- Weekly rank is descriptive, not a measure of drafting ability or a reconstructed lineup outcome.
- Do not merge former historical Evans or Koble years into later managers; **Evans played in 2023, not JMid**.
- Keep 2026 explicitly provisional through Week 8 only.
