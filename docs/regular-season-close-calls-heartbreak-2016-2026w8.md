## Publication safeguards

1. Separate regular-season records from *championship-bracket playoff* records. The same winning margin means something different in those contexts.
2. Do not claim a game changed playoff seeding or the championship outcome without a verified standings/playoff counterfactual. In particular, do not infer the story of a fumble just from one small margin.
3. Keep the 2016 tie distinct from a decisive win by zero points; a tie is not a 0.00 victory.
4. Exclude 2026 from completed-season all-time leaderboards until full season reconciliation; report only its explicit Week 8 snapshot.
5. Avoid labeling a high-scoring loss "bad management" or a low-scoring victory "genius". These describe outcomes and schedule fortune, not decision quality.

**Editorial fit:** Choose a small `Cruelest Losses & Narrowest Escapes` section in the Record Book or a single source-backed profile anecdote per manager. Do not create ten additional top-level menu links.
