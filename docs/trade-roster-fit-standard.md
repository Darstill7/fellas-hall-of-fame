# Roster fit and decision quality

For every archived trade reconstruct both managers' full roster immediately before and after the transaction using timestamped transactions and historical snapshots. Weekly snapshots are anchors, not proof of exact trade-time ownership. Identify position eligibility, starter and FLEX competition, bench depth, bye and injury exposure only as known at the time, standings and keeper considerations. Do not infer motives from positional counts alone; distinguish manager-reported rationale from analyst inference. Evaluate outgoing-player opportunity cost as well as incoming upgrades. Compare legal lineups with and without the exchange using the same information set and scoring horizon; separate contemporaneous decision quality from realized hindsight. Hold unrelated transactions constant and flag unavailable counterfactual points. No perfect-hindsight lineup should be called an actual manager decision. Record roster fit separately and integrate it within the existing 30% lineup impact component, with no new weight or double counting until approved calibration. Both managers may receive favorable grades. Missing snapshots, exact ordering, or alternative evidence block definitive lineup-impact grades. For Week 1 use draft plus preseason transaction reconstruction, not a fabricated Week 0 snapshot. All 57 trade articles require this review before final approval.
