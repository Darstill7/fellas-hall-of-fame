# The Fellas — The Other 26 Historical Trades
**Reopened research:** November 16, 2026. **Status:** Audit-grade historical receipts, not certified PAR or trade-skill rankings. **Scope:** The 26 of 44 documented 2020–2025 trade events absent from the 18 fully name-matched bilateral PAR preview.

## Bottom line
There are **23 transactions with both player sides documented**, **one verified three-manager deal**, and **two completed trades whose counterparties can now be named from provider transaction receipts but whose return asset sides remain unknown**. Calling these simply `incomplete` hides existing evidence. Every named player asset across the 26 has now been searched in the original annual player-start archive. The sole player with no annual starter identity, **Kenny Gainwell (2023)**, has independent Sleeper player ID **7567** in the original completed transaction receipt; **zero recorded starts is not an identity failure**.
**Headline caution:** the 26 entries were absent from the earlier **position-adjusted PAR sample**. The numbers below are **actual regular-season starting points credited to the acquiring manager in weeks strictly AFTER the trade through Week 14**. They are not replacement-adjusted, exclude transaction-week starts and playoffs, and may be unfair to acquisitions with keeper upside. The older `v9_trade_engine` often reports larger figures under a different or unclear aggregation scope; those older values have not been silently overridden.

## Newly surfaced deal analyses
### 2021 Evans versus Kuhl: the missing big deal
Evans received **Aaron Rodgers and Keenan Allen**, who supplied **325.40** regular-season post-trade started points on the receiving roster (Rodgers 204.80; Allen 120.60, 18 combined starts). Kuhl received **Leonard Fournette, Patrick Mahomes and Brandon Aiyuk**, who supplied **180.48** (Mahomes 178.48; Aiyuk 2.00; Fournette no recorded starts on Kuhl after the deal). The **+144.92 raw-point edge toward Evans** makes this essential to any balanced future Kuhl career assessment. It does not overturn Kuhl's +243.62 PAR in his four *other fully qualified trades*; a position-adjusted calculation and keeper valuation are required.
### 2023 Stew versus Birch: the CeeDee Lamb exchange
Stew received **Tony Pollard and Jaxon Smith-Njigba** (47.90 post-trade starter points). Birch received **CeeDee Lamb and Kenny Gainwell** (163.10, all from Lamb; Gainwell has no recorded starts). **Birch +115.20 raw points**. Gainwell is now identified from the original Sleeper transaction as player ID **7567**. This is a candidate for one of Birch's strongest practical acquisitions; raw points do not identify the legal replacement player Stew would otherwise have started.
### 2021 Stover versus Kuhl: Leonard Fournette for DeVonta Smith
Stover received **154.60** from Fournette across eight post-trade starts; Kuhl received **46.10** from Smith across six. **Stover +108.50 raw points**. This acquisition needs to appear alongside the 2026 Trevor Lawrence trade in Stover's trading history; it was missing from the narrow historical qualification sample.
### 2024 Tony versus Thommy: a three-for-two
Tony received **Cooper Kupp, Kareem Hunt and Rico Dowdle**, who contributed **178.80** across 13 post-trade starts. Thommy received **Travis Kelce and Brandon Aiyuk**, who contributed **78.70** across seven; Aiyuk contributed no *post-trade* recorded starts. **Tony +100.10 raw started points**. In the original engine the figures are 216.90 and 107.30; the narrower date scope explains why a source-locked audit is necessary.
### 2020 Evans versus Stew: Ronald Jones for A.J. Green
Evans acquired **Ronald Jones** and received **82.40** points from six post-trade starts. Stew's **A.J. Green** supplied **2.90** in one start. **Evans +79.50 raw points**. Both sides are now name-and-ID identifiable in the 2020 season-start file even though the earlier PAR preview excluded Ronald Jones.
### 2024 JMid versus Stew: Kyren Williams, Calvin Ridley and James Cook
JMid received **Kyren Williams and Calvin Ridley**: **180.70** started points after the trade, all supplied by Kyren. Stew received **James Cook III**: **139.90** in the same strict window. This is a **+40.80 raw gap for JMid**, but only 11 recorded starts for each side; a normalized RB positional comparison may produce a different verdict.
### 2020 Stover versus Stew: a genuine cross-ledger discrepancy
The older trade engine credits **45.00** to Stover's D.J. Moore + Clyde Edwards-Helaire side. The week-by-week archive yields **81.20** post-trade starter points (**45.00 DJ Moore + 36.20 Edwards-Helaire**), versus **53.10** for Stew's Michael Thomas. The apparent raw edge flips from **Stew +8.10 in the older engine** to **Stover +28.10 in the strictly reconstructed archive**. This is a **reconciliation blocker**, not a correction to publish as an established skill grade.
### 2025 Stew versus Kuhl: the late-season trade can flip by window
The older engine reports **Stew 114.80** for Jake Ferguson + Jaylen Warren versus **Kuhl 128.80** for Zach Ertz + Derrick Henry. Using only *later* regular-season weeks: **Stew 59.90**, **Kuhl 50.40**. Thus the numerical side with more raw points **reverses** between sources. This illustrates why transaction-week and playoff attribution must be verified before a lifetime win-loss score is released.
### 2023 Week 14 Evans versus Kuhl: regular-season PAR cannot grade the deal
Evans received Ja'Marr Chase, Kuhl Tyler Lockett. Both total **zero strictly AFTER Week 14** because the archived regular season ends at Week 14. The older event engine does record **14.00** versus **15.70**, apparently in a different window. That is not a regular-season tie or a bad trade; the postseason and keeper windows need their own audit.
### 2025 three-manager trade: a confirmed major lineage event
Stilly received Amon-Ra St. Brown, Jordan Addison and Jared Goff (**91.56 post-trade regular-season starter points**); JMid acquired Ja'Marr Chase (**23.20**); Thommy acquired A.J. Brown (**64.20**). Older trade archives carry larger totals (**199.72**, **78.30**, **98.60**), illustrating window differences. The correct analysis is an asset-flow and keeper/postseason study, not a single bilateral margin.

## Why the first leaderboard must NOT be replaced by an unsourced 44-deal PAR ranking
1. The previous 18 deals have position-relative PAR numbers; the 23 newly examined bilateral deals presently have **reconciled started points**, not the same baseline-normalized PAR model. The two metrics cannot be summed.
2. The original engine's wider started-point windows differ from the strict regular-season week-after-trade window, sometimes reversing the apparent winner (2020 Stew–Stover and 2025 Stew–Kuhl). Reconciliation needs a dated scope contract and verified playoff receipt.
3. A player with zero recorded starts may have been benched, traded later, injured, or retained as a keeper. Zero contribution is not necessarily zero asset value.
4. Replacement-player and roster constraints are necessary to claim an additional fantasy victory, not simply subtracting the traded player's points.
5. The 2023 ownership correction assigns all relevant manager activity on slot 2 to **Evans**, not JMid, even if source account handles differ.
