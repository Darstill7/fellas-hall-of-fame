## Definitions

- **Consecutive over 100:** Team scored *strictly more than 100.00* fantasy points in successive recorded regular-season weeks. An exact 100.00 **does not count**.
- **Consecutive at least 105:** Team scored **105.00 or more** in successive weeks. These are different records and must not be conflated.
- **Season boundaries stop a streak**; do not manufacture a bridge from one season's last week to the next season's first. Postseason games are excluded.
- **Missing data stops a documented streak:** the unified 2020/2021 matchup archive has two teams each without a paired Week 14 game score. Their streak evidence can be understated. This is **not** proof those teams scored zero, had a bye, or failed to reach a threshold.
- Scoring formats changed across eras. This is a descriptive week-to-week consistency result, not an era-adjusted manager skill ranking.

## Coverage caveats and quality gates

- Recompute from the canonical weekly matchup archive with a regular-season stage filter; do not include championships, bye scores or consolation results.
- Never join disjoint weeks; gaps in the source must remain unknown and break a *verified* consecutive-run calculation.
- Distinguish records through completed seasons **2016–2025** from provisional **2026 through Week 8**; the latter may change.
- Maintain the distinct `>100` and `>=105` criteria, including exact-boundary cases.
- Do not compare the absolute totals to older ESPN eras as if scoring systems were identical.

**Recommended display:** A modest `Scoring Consistency` section in the Record Book, showing verified lengths and precise season/week ranges without speculative awards or manager skill ratings.
