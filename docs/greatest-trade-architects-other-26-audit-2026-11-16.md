# Greatest Trade Architects — The Other 26 Trades

**Research audit · November 16, 2026**

This appendix reviews **all 26** 2020–2025 trade events excluded from the initial **18-deal fully qualified PAR sample**. The original trade engine has **44 distinct recorded transactions** for these six seasons. Exclusion from the PAR comparison did **not** mean the underlying trade lacked assets or starter scores.

**What can be reconstructed:** 23 bilateral trades have player assets and a strict post-trade Week 14 regular-season starter tally for each recorded side; one 2025 three-manager exchange has all three recipient sides; two cases have only one documented asset-receiving side. Both teams may be known for those two, but the complete exchange is not.

**Do not conflate the columns.** “Archived total” is the older trade engine’s recorded recipient starting points (not necessarily limited to strictly later regular-season weeks). “Strict post-trade” joins received players to their documented starter entries in the season's weekly archive, in **weeks after** the transaction through Week 14. Neither is PAR or a legal replacement-lineup counterfactual; differences can reflect same-week starts, later playoffs, historical coverage or alias mismatches. These deltas are diagnostic, not evidence of wrongdoing or a manager's skill.

Names have been normalized only for clear punctuation/suffix variants, with the original player and roster IDs retained in the [machine-readable source audit](../data-integrity/trade-architects-unqualified-26-research-2026-11-16.json). An absence of post-trade starts is a measured zero, **not** missing player identity and **not** automatically a bad trade.

## Priority reconstruction findings

- **2021 Evans–Kuhl, Aaron Rodgers + Keenan Allen vs Leonard Fournette + Patrick Mahomes + Brandon Aiyuk:** Evans-side strict later starter total 325.40 vs Kuhl's 180.48. Some parts of the player history may have been transferred again, so no permanent keeper or skill verdict follows.
- **2023 Stew–Birch, Tony Pollard + Jaxon Smith-Njigba vs CeeDee Lamb + Kenny Gainwell:** Birch's strict starter return 163.10 vs Stew's 47.90. The other acquired player, Kenny Gainwell, has no 2023 season starter identity in the archive; its zero is an observed lack of credited starts, not proof he wasn't rostered.
- **2021 Stover–Kuhl, Leonard Fournette vs DeVonta Smith:** strict regular-season post-trade started points 154.60 vs 46.10. A worthwhile historical championship/playoff impact candidate, but not an apples-to-apples positional comparison.
- **2024 Thommy–Tony, Kelce + Aiyuk vs Kupp + Hunt + Dowdle:** strict starter returns 78.70 vs 178.80. Aiyuk produced no eligible post-trade starts, and the potential player-health/keeper decisions need separate context.
- **2025 Stew–Birch, Quentin Johnston vs Bhayshul Tuten:** 64.50 vs 0.00 post-trade starter points, but the zero is not enough to declare a poor ex-ante keeper bet.
- **2025 Stilly–JMid–Thommy three-manager transaction:** strictly later 2025 regular-season observed starters: Stilly 91.56, JMid 23.20, Thommy 64.20. Older broader scoring totals differ; the long-term keeper transfer of Amon-Ra St. Brown is material. Treat as three distinct benefit streams, **not** a three-way “win percentage.”

## Reconciliation priorities

1. **Temporal parity:** compare old source totals with same-week + post-trade regular-season plus playoff-scoring coverage before declaring a mismatch. Some older totals likely include the transaction week and postseason. Cross-check exact transaction timestamps versus lineup locks.
2. **Player-ID matching:** validate mismatching spellings (DJ Moore / D.J. Moore; Allen Robinson / Allen Robinson II). Use IDs and verified ownership before introducing inferred transfers.
3. **One-sided asset receipts:** 2020 W8 George Kittle to Stilly (transaction 629416763662086144) and 2021 W3 AJ Dillon to Stilly (748585137259884544) still need documented opposite asset sides. Do not invent a trade partner package.
4. **PAR rebuild:** apply one verified position-relative methodology consistently to all 41 documented bilateral trades (18 original qualifiers plus 23 recovered), withholding awards until that same method passes cross-checks and excludes unverified trade components.
5. **Manager identity:** Evans owned the 2023 slot, not JMid. The playoff and keeper treatment must honor the commissioner correction; there are no certified 2016–2019 ESPN player-start trade comparators yet.
6. **Historical scope:** 44 previously represented 2020–2025 records are the sample in the old engine, not proof of *all* trades made across those seasons. Review provider transaction completeness and reconcile any unregistered trades before claiming complete coverage.
