## Distinguish three questions

1. **Production:** actual verified starts/points for the original manager.
2. **Round-slot return:** keeper original-manager starter points minus median starter points from nonkeeper picks in the same draft round. An after-the-fact proxy, **not** an alternative that was available at keeper lock.
3. **Decision quality:** requires all eligible keepers and actual draft-round opportunity cost known at keeper lock. Not presently determined.

## Minimum evidence checklist (per manager)

- [ ] Snapshot of full roster at keeper lock (not the roster after draft-day moves)
- [ ] Player eligibility according to the specific season's keeper rules and prior acquisition history
- [ ] Each eligible player's actual keeper round cost, escalation, and any drop/trade penalties
- [ ] Traded draft selections and ownership of affected rounds at the deadline
- [ ] Timestamped projections or ranks available **before** keeper lock (ex ante assessment)
- [ ] Explicit chosen keepers and keeper-lock date
- [ ] Original-manager starter outcomes and post-trade handling for ex post comparison

**Publication gate:** Do not assign a letter, an alternative-player identity, a decision score, or opportunity-cost amount without the underlying eligibility evidence. Missing values must remain null/unavailable; do not treat a nonkeeper same-round median as the option foregone.

## Method once eligibility is certified

For each eligible keeper candidate, estimate decision-time projected starter value net of the draft pick foregone and keeper slot used. Compare only feasible combinations of exactly two keeper choices for that manager, taking account of round conflicts and traded picks. Archive the pre-decision projection and confidence separately from realized return. Show both the ex ante decision rationale and ex post outcome; do not rank players retrospectively by later results and call that good drafting.

## Historical limitations

The 2025 observed keeper production is documented, but historical eligibility and rules are not fully certified. Do not infer a full keeper selection process from 2023 or earlier records. Player acquisitions after keeper lock must never enter the alternative set.
