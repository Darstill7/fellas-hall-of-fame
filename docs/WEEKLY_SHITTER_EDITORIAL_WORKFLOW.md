# Weekly Shitter editorial workflow
1. Collect finalized results and reconcile canonical standings. Never label incomplete weeks final.
2. Generate the research packet from the approved week. CI artifacts contain public league facts only.
3. Commissioner supplies informal observations, corrections, group-chat context, personal angles and private background directly in chat. No required form. Never commit private notes or pass them into public CI artifacts.
4. Verify factual claims against source receipts; preserve unverified allegations as questions or omit. Private background is not for publication.
5. Separately gather same-cutoff playoff simulations, trade receipts, archived writer predictions, lineup projections, injuries, byes and next-week matchups. Do not invent unavailable facts or rewrite original predictions.
6. Draft five matchup roasts, trade analysis, playoff picture, Roast the Writer, upcoming picks and Final Flush, preserving the approved dark typography-led magazine identity and clearly fictional satire/sponsors.
7. Commissioner can revise the draft before article publication. No automatic article or group message.
 
The initial engine handles scores, season standings, weekly scoring ranks, all-play, close games, high-scoring losers and low-scoring winners. Remaining editorial evidence adapters are follow-up work. Local notes JSON may use kinds observation, correction, angle, private_background; keep private inputs and output local, not in GitHub.
