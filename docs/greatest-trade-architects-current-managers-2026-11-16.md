## Three *different* measures; never blend them
1. **Qualified historical PAR (2020–2025)**: only 18 fully matched two-manager deals, archived position-relative post-trade starter PAR. Incomplete sample. Existing source `data/trade_par_preview.json`; source `data/trade_winners_losers_research_2020_2025_v1.json`.
2. **All recorded historical position-median sensitivity (2020–2025)**: all 41 two-manager trades, each starting score of an acquired player in *strictly later* regular-season weeks 1–14 minus the median among all same-position actual starters that week. Includes every named player even when he generated no such starts. This is **NOT** the original PAR measure, replacement value, forward-looking player valuation or causal skill evidence. Source `data-integrity/trade-architects-all44-position-median-sensitivity-2026-11-16.json`.
3. **Current 2026 raw points**: nine completed trades across Weeks 1–10, acquired starter points (not position adjusted). Week 10 is still live and not final. Source: connected 2026 Sleeper completed trade activity and matchup team starters.
