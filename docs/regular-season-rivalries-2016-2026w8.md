# Rivalries by the receipts — Fellas regular-season head-to-head research

**Scope:** Completed regular seasons 2016–2025 plus the **2026 Week 8 snapshot** in `data/season_weekly_matchups_2016_2026_v1.json`. Research staged, **not yet published as a Record Book entry**. Only the ten current managers appear in this current-manager view, but former managers' games remain preserved separately in the canonical archive. Rivalry stats use *regular-season head-to-head meetings only*: no championship, semifinal, consolation or hypothetical schedule games.

## The league's longest-running verified head-to-head

**Stilly vs Stew: 20 regular-season meetings, split exactly 10–10.** Across those matchups, Stilly scored **2,035.16** total fantasy points and Stew **2,016.78**—only **18.38 points** difference over the whole rivalry (across changing scoring environments). Their latest recovered result was Stilly **124.62–105.98** Stew in **2026 Week 4**. The closest was **2021 Week 14**, a **111.32–108.94 Stew win** (2.38 points).

That is a better narrative than simply calling one manager a rival without evidence. It is also a strictly **regular-season 10–10 tie**, not a combined regular-season-and-playoff series.

## Other verified story angles

- **Stover over Thommy:** 11–5 in 16 regular-season meetings, with a **six-meeting** head-to-head winning run from 2021 Week 5 through 2024 Week 8. That does **not** include playoff meetings or imply a six-week streak.
- **Mitch over Stilly:** 11–4 across 15 regular-season games; six straight head-to-head wins from 2023 Week 1 through 2026 Week 7. This pattern does not itself prove any championship-seeding counterfactual.
- **Kuhl vs Stover:** a second series tied **7–7** through 14 recorded meetings, despite an especially lopsided latest game (Stover 129.26–56.32 Kuhl in 2026 Week 2).
- **Stew vs Stover:** 17 games, Stover leads **9–8**; Stover assembled five consecutive head-to-head wins from 2022 Week 11 through 2025 Week 5 before Stew's 2026 Week 8 victory.

## Essential caveats

- The 2026 schedule is only reconciled through Week 8. No 2026 Week 9 result is implied.
- Original historical manager ownership matters: **Evans, not JMid, controlled the 2023 slot**. Do not merge their statistics or claim JMid played earlier games.
- A six-win head-to-head streak means **six successive times those two managers met**; matches could be months or years apart.
- A 20-meeting cumulative point spread is informative but not an era-neutral performance rating, because league rules and scoring systems may have changed.
- This package includes each match in the source JSON for reproducibility. A profile claim may link to a source receipt only after a separate UI quality pass.

**Recommended editorial use:** Feature the Stilly–Stew deadlock as a single rivalry profile highlight. Keep other numbers accessible in a modest expanded head-to-head research section rather than overcrowding ten manager home pages.
