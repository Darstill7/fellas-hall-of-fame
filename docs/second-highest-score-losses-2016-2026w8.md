# Second-best that week. Still lost.

**Staged Fellas research, November 9, 2026**. The question is simple: *How often did a manager finish second in scoring across the entire league but lose to the team ranked first?*

## Headline

The recovered **2016–2025 completed-season archive has 16 such losses** in fully paired regular-season weeks. **Stilly has the most, with four.** Stew follows with three. No such result occurred in the 2026 Week 1–8 paired snapshot.

This is the specific kind of opponent-draw frustration that a season-total points-for leaderboard cannot capture. It is **not** evidence that those managers made bad lineup decisions; in fact, they outscored every eligible team *other* than the one they faced.

## Evidence boundaries

- Only count **fully paired regular-season weeks** with every league team represented. In 2016 there were eight teams; subsequently ten.
- Incomplete 2020–2021 Week 14 cannot be assigned complete weekly ranks. Exclude it for the entire league in those seasons instead of pretending the remaining eight scores reflect the whole field.
- If scores tie, use competition ranking, with a head-to-head tie never treated as a loss.
- 2026 remains through Week 8 and must not be added to historical completed-season rankings without re-running source validation.
- This is a descriptive schedule matchup result, not a formal 'luck grade' or a playoff-seeding counterfactual.
- Preserve Evans' original historical participation; do not assign his earlier years to JMid.
