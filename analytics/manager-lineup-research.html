How to read this: Higher percentages mean the starting lineup scored closer to the best legal lineup available from the recorded roster that week. This measures results with hindsight, not the quality of choices made before kickoff.
How is accuracy calculated?

We reconstruct weekly lineup accuracy from archived starters and eligible bench players. Sleeper does not provide a simple historical league-wide accuracy endpoint. Our Week 8, 2026 comparison matched Stilly at 92.4% and Kuhl at 83.1%, supporting the calculation, but missing or incomplete roster evidence is excluded rather than estimated.
