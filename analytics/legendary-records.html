What counts as an official Fellas record?

These achievements were checked against recovered ESPN and Sleeper regular-season results, the canonical season guide and independently verified championship brackets. They do not award invented points for trades, lineup decisions, or hypothetical outcomes.

League size and playoff structure changed, and point scoring has differed across eras. Finalized championship and playoff outcomes cover 2016–2025; 2026 remains unfinished in this dataset. The 2023 franchise belongs to Evans, while JMid joined in 2024.
