Two different kinds of value

Both measure actual documented contribution; neither should be presented as a final drafting-skill grade while the season is active.

Draft Pick Value

For a normal pick, compare what the player contributed to your starters against other nonkeeper picks made in the same round.

Value Points = Starter Points − Same-Round Peer Median

Value Points per Pick is the average of those differences across your nonkeeper picks. This credits late-round contributors like Woody Marks without artificially dividing by a zero-point baseline.

Keeper Slot Return

For a keeper, compare realized lineup points against the median nonkeeper selected in the same round. The retained round represents the recorded cost.

Keeper Slot Surplus = Keeper Starter Points − Nonkeeper Round Median

Keeper Decision Quality is different. To judge whether keeping a player was the smartest choice, we first need evidence of each manager's eligible alternatives and keeper costs at the deadline. No invented decision grade.
