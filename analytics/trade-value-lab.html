Trade Value Lab

A better comparison than total raw points: production from acquired players after the trade, measured against same-position starter benchmarks. Click a trade to inspect the asset-level evidence.

Research preview, not definitive trade grades. Post-trade weeks begin after the transaction week, which avoids ambiguous same-week ownership. Only exact-name assets with recorded starts can be matched. Zero documented starts, draft picks, keeper implications, and incomplete ownership are not treated as zero value. A trade is comparable only when both sides have complete matched asset evidence; even then, position-adjusted scoring is not the same as a full trade winner verdict.
