Separate research lane

Keeper value vs. keeper decisions

Measured today

Kept player's verified original-manager lineup starts and starter points, recorded round cost, descriptive performance versus starters at the same position, and for 2026 a separate observed round-slot return versus nonkeepers selected in that same round.

Not yet defensible

True keeper opportunity cost and a decision grade require the eligible alternatives available at keeper lock. The observed round-slot return is a useful after-the-fact comparison, not proof that another player was available or should have been kept.

ManagerRecorded keepersStarter ptsVs. positional medianDecision grade
Exactly what would unlock opportunity-cost grades

We need the complete keeper-deadline eligible roster for each manager, every player's eligibility and round cost under that year's rules, traded draft pick ownership where relevant, and an agreed comparison standard. Only then can we measure the alternatives forfeited by the keeper choices. No missing alternative is treated as zero.
