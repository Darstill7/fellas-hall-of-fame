Official all-time career record · Schedule Luck

Birch · +6.34 wins above expectation

Across nine completed seasons (120 fully paired regular-season weeks), Birch earned 67 actual win credits against 60.665 all-play expected wins. That is the greatest favorable cumulative schedule draw in the verified league archive through 2025.

Schedule Luck = actual wins (ties count as half) minus expected wins against a randomly drawn opponent from the same league week. This measures opponent-draw fortune, not managerial skill; eligibility covers fully paired weeks and includes former managers. For fair comparisons across different tenures, also see the per-week schedule advantage.
