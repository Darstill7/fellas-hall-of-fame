What qualifies as a trade verdict?

No voided-pick shortcuts.

The value is in the actual player exchange. The infamous 2023 Jefferson/Jacobs trade has a separate historical story, but we will not award a “worst trade ever” trophy merely because the league later voided traded draft picks. Draft-pick governance and player-return outcomes must be assessed separately.

What the research measures
  • Both managers' received players are matched to archived starting-lineup production.
  • Position-relative points above baseline (PAR) count only later regular-season starts after the transaction week.
  • The recipient with the greater observed PAR has the measured advantage, not necessarily the stronger total trade package or better process.
  • Playoff impact, keeper rights, FAAB, draft-pick value, injuries, replacement options, and subsequent trades need separate research.
What is still excluded
  • 2016–2019 ESPN-era deals have not been brought to the same evidence standard.
  • Three-team deals, including the 2025 keeper-shaping trade, need a separate multi-party model.
  • Unmatched asset identities and unfinished 2026 outcomes do not qualify for definitive rankings.

Primary data: audited research registry. These rankings are not definitive all-time rankings of every trade since the 2016 league origin.
