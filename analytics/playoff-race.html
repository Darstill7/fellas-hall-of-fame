Toughest remaining schedules

Which teams have the toughest games left? This rating combines opponents’ season scoring with their recent scoring. Higher means tougher.

How is schedule difficulty calculated?

The rating uses 70% of opponents’ season scoring averages and 30% of their last-three-week scoring averages. Each remaining matchup counts equally. All-play excludes equal scores. Recent scoring uses completed weeks only. Rankings use difficulty rounded to two decimals; equal ratings share a rank. This is a relative schedule rating, not a predicted score or a win probability. No injury or player projections are included.
