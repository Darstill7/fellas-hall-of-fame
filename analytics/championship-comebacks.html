Record 01 / Worst opening start by a champion

The impossible comeback

0–4Opening four weeks · 2023

Stilly · 2023 championship season

Four weeks into 2023, Stilly had yet to win a game. By the end of the season, he owned the championship.

The archived season shows a 0–4 start, a 1–5 mark after Week 6, and an eventual 8–6 regular-season finish. Stilly entered the playoffs as the fourth seed and completed the title run. The record is specifically about consecutive losses to open a championship season, not the total number of losses.

Record 02 / Deepest deficit survived by a champion

Four games under. One title above.

−4Games below .500 · 2023

Stilly · 2023 championship season

That same championship season also owns a separate distinction: the largest deficit below .500 that an eventual Fellas champion recovered from.

The distinction matters. An opening losing streak measures how a season began; the deficit record measures how far behind even a champion fell at any point. Stilly hit that depth at 0–4 and again at 1–5. This is a separate record category describing the same remarkable season, not an additional championship.

Record 03 / Lowest playoff seed to win the championship

The Cinderella champion

No. 6Championship seed · 2020

Koble · 2020 champion

The 2020 regular season ended at 7–7. Koble entered the postseason as the sixth seed and walked out with the championship.

This is a playoff-seeding record, not a claim that Koble had the lowest scoring team or the worst championship roster. It measures the largest official winners-bracket seed number held by a champion across the verified championship brackets.
