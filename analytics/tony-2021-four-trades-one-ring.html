The decisive distinction

They built the score. The entire lineup won the title.

Mixon's 29.50 was Tony's highest championship starter score, but Davante Adams contributed 28.40, Tampa Bay's defense scored 19.00, and Alexander Mattison added 14.50. Those were not part of the four verified incoming trade-player links. A 4.30-point winning margin makes the acquired production especially memorable. It does not mean Tony would have lost by 58.60 points without those trades: alternative starters would have had their own scores, and the roster itself would have differed.

The defensible conclusion is still striking. Four distinct transactions delivered four starting pieces of a championship team. Those players supplied 112.10 points over two postseason victories. Tony didn't inherit an undefeated machine. He turned a four-win season into a 10–3 regular-season finish and a trophy.

Historical designation: A fully documented four-acquisition championship construction case—not a claim that this is the greatest trade strategy across every season since 2016.
