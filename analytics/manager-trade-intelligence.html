Source evidence and classification

Each event is grouped by year and archived trade ID. Only nonprovisional trade sides with confidence starting with “verified” and numeric direct-edge values qualify. A multi-party event is treated as represented only if at least two different managers are present and exactly one certified side exists for each of them; this is a structural check, not proof that every participant in the original Sleeper transaction is represented. We do not deduce missing players, pick transfers, keeper values, or current team needs.
