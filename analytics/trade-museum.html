How are trade statistics calculated?

Points shown are historical audit snapshots, NOT continuously updated season totals. For example, the October 7, 2026 Stilly/Ja’Marr Chase trade shows 5.7 points at its audit checkpoint even though later starts added more points. Where available, scores represent source-audited starter production credited to acquired players. Newly verified exchanges can appear with Not audited values until production is independently calculated. These are not adjusted for positional scarcity, keeper/draft assets, alternate lineups, or trade-time expectations. Some historical trades have incomplete assets or intentionally ungraded sides. For more context, explore Trade History. The 2026 season is still in progress.

The main collection shows documented fully represented multi-team player exchanges. One-sided or genuinely unverified exchanges are archived separately. Three-team trades are valid transactions and appear here when all participant sides are documented. FAAB may be part of a trade even when not represented by these player-only receipts; keeper eligibility follows the traded player. Draft-pick trading is banned under current rules.
