04 · Directly sourced production

Received starter points — no replacement assumptions

An independently reconstructed, zero-sum accounting of points actually scored in the receiving manager’s lineup after each trade and before that season’s playoffs. Unlike experimental PAR, the underlying weekly points allow us to correctly remove Week 14 playoff appearances from five additional 2020–2021 trades. The raw sample includes 18 fully matched trades, or 24 with six provisional zero-start receipts, independently of the 13/19 experimental PAR comparison above. This measure does not normalize QB/RB/WR/TE or FLEX opportunities, value unstarted or kept players, or establish who negotiated better. Its manager/year and optional recovery filters follow the research sample above; the underlying player-start points are source-reconciled.

06 · Audit trail

Every trade behind the number

Method: each eligible transaction contributes side A postTradePAR − side B postTradePAR, with the sign reversed for side B. Inherited PAR estimates use started production after the transaction week; this display excludes any entire trade whose source includes starters at or beyond its independently verified first playoff week (Week 14 in 2020–2021, Week 15 in 2022–2025). The weekly replacement formula remains experimental and unverified. The same trade is counted once per pair and once per manager; no component is counted twice. The data is research-scoped and may be superseded when more source receipts are certified.
