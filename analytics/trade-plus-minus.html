Research queue · Not graded

Trades awaiting production matching

Priority research: cases with just one unresolved received asset may be easier to investigate, but they are not necessarily identity mismatches; a player may have had no verified eligible post-trade starter appearance. These 23 two-manager receipts remain excluded from the +/- rankings. “Unmatched” means the archived PAR preview cannot certify a later starter-production match for a received asset; it does not prove the player had zero value or that an identity was entered incorrectly. Resolve player identity, roster ownership, week eligibility, and box-score provenance before changing any grade.

04 · Audit trail

Every trade behind the number

Method: each eligible transaction contributes side A postTradePAR − side B postTradePAR, with the sign reversed for side B. PAR measures strictly later regular-season starter utility relative to position, through Week 14. The same trade is counted once per pair and once per manager; no component is counted twice. The data is research-scoped and may be superseded when more source receipts are certified.
