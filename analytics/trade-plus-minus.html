Who got the better
of whom?

A directional trade +/- between managers, based on position-relative fantasy starter production after each fully audited two-manager trade. Every number is traceable to the original archived research, not a judgment about anyone's negotiating ability.

Measured trade advantage, not total trade value. This is the qualifying 2020–2025 sample, not an exhaustive all-time history. Only trades where both acquired sides were fully matched are included. The measure excludes 2016–2019, 2026, incomplete and three-manager receipts, postseason results, keeper rights, FAAB, and roster counterfactuals. A missing pairing means not measured, not a draw or zero.

03 · Audit trail

Every trade behind the number

Method: each eligible transaction contributes side A postTradePAR − side B postTradePAR, with the sign reversed for side B. PAR measures strictly later regular-season starter utility relative to position, through Week 14. The same trade is counted once per pair and once per manager; no component is counted twice. The data is research-scoped and may be superseded when more source receipts are certified.
