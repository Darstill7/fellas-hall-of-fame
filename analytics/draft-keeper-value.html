Methodology and evidence

All metrics are reproducible from archived draft picks and exact starting-lineup receipts. They are research indicators, not changes to the official Hall of Fame OVR or historical records.

How Draft Pick Value is calculated

Draft Pick Value = original manager's started points − median started points from nearby same-position, same-season draft selections. Peers start within two draft rounds; if fewer than four qualifying picks exist, the search widens to three, then five, then all rounds for that position. A pick is excluded from its own baseline. Counts and the peer median are shown so the comparison stays inspectable.

A late-round pick with zero nearby peer starter production can show a positive return equal to all of its actual starter points. That is a valid descriptive statistic, but does not prove the pick would have been the best available player at the draft.

How Keeper Return differs from Keeper Decision

Realized Keeper Return credits original-manager starts and their points, then subtracts the same-position starter median for the weeks actually started. The keeper's round cost is displayed independently.

Keeper Decision Value would compare every eligible keeper candidate, the expected return at the specific keeper-slot cost, the remaining draft alternatives, and the resulting opportunity cost using only information that existed at the decision deadline. Full alternative inventories are not yet established, so decision grades remain unavailable.

Historical and partial-season limitations

2025 is completed; 2026 is through verified Week 8. The 2026 draft engine has original-manager starter scoring for Weeks 1–6, with Weeks 7–8 appended from archived team starting-lineup snapshots. Scoring after a trade for another manager, bench points and projections are not included. Keeper-flagged selections are excluded from the open-market draft leaderboard. Earlier keeper rules were not confirmed before 2025, so we do not retroactively award pre-2025 keeper value.
