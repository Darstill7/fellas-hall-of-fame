Madden-Style Manager Cards

An overall rating (OVR) built from actual league success, with scouting-style attributes alongside it. Weekly ratings are recomputed from archived matchups through the selected week. Unknown attributes show N/A—not invented scores. Each manager earns one primary archetype and can earn additional specialties independently, based on documented achievements—not a subjective personality assessment.

Two different measures: OVR is the verified success-first all-time index, not an average of the attributes below. Drafting and trading are evidence-limited estimates; waivers and start/sit remain ungraded. OVR snapshots use 2026 weeks through the selected date; scouting attributes below remain current archive-wide assessments and do not rewind with the OVR. 2026 is not a completed season.

What do the manager ratings mean? (Rating guide)
OVR — Overall Rating
The complete success-first score out of 100.0, based on championships, playoffs, wins, all-play performance, recent form, peak seasons and longevity. It is not an average of the attributes below.
Regular-season winning
Recorded wins, losses and ties, adjusted for sample size.
All-play performance
How often the team would have beaten the other league teams in the same week, regardless of its scheduled opponent.
Playoff success
Verified appearances in the league's championship playoff bracket, with recent and repeated qualifications rewarded.
Championships
Verified league championships, with more recent titles carrying more current-rating weight.
Championship game appearances
Verified runner-up finishes. Championships are scored separately.
Drafting
Estimated value from documented non-keeper draft picks, compared with other managers. Provisional.
Trading
Estimated value from verified trade records. Provisional.
Waiver pickups
How effective free-agent and waiver acquisitions were. N/A until full decision evidence is available.
Start/sit decisions
How effectively managers choose weekly starters. N/A until lineup decision evidence is sufficient.
N/A — Not Available
Insufficient verified data to assign a fair score, not a zero or a poor grade.
