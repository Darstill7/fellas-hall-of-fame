Methodology

How Draft Utility Is Graded

Actual output

Fellas starter points

A player earns draft utility only when his points are actually put into the drafting manager’s lineup. Playoff starter points are shown separately so non-playoff teams are not penalized.

Round adjusted

Surplus vs round median

Regular-season starter points minus the completed-era median for that draft round. This prevents a productive Round 2 pick from automatically outranking a league-changing Round 14 pick.

Asset handling

TRADED and KEEPER

A drafted asset traded away that season is not called a bust; its value is evaluated in the Trade Engine. Keeper slots are identified separately. 2026 grades are provisional pace grades through Week 6.
