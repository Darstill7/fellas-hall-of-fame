The limitations matter

These measures use fully paired weeks, excluding missing 2020–2021 Week 14 matchups. The 2016 league had eight teams, so all-play comparisons use seven possible opponents; later seasons had ten teams and nine alternatives. The finished 2026 season is unavailable.

Opponent points exposure measures actual opposition relative to the week's average alternative opponent. It does not imply an injured player, a bench decision, or a trade caused any result. Past 2023 franchise ownership belongs to Evans rather than JMid.
