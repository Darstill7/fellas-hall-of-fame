What the tracker can—and cannot—tell us

Confirmed: Chosen keeper names and the round paid in official drafts. The 2026 Week 8 original-manager starting points come from source-backed draft research; bench points and post-trade starts are not credited.

Conditional: Next-season cost is one round earlier if the manager retains the player and the player is eligible. A projected Round 1 cost is the last calculable cost; anything earlier is marked as requiring league clarification. If a player was a 2025 keeper for the same manager, we can confirm at least two consecutive keeper seasons in 2026, but without a fully verified 2024 keeper history we do not assert how many years remain.

Not yet established: The exact eligible alternatives at keeper lock, everyone’s current three-year counter, first-round escalation behavior, same-round conflicts, and whether every candidate survived offseason roster changes. Those gaps prevent a legitimate Keeper Decision Grade.
