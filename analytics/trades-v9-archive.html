Methodology

What This Engine Measures

Direct utility

Starter points delivered

Only points actually placed into the acquiring manager’s starting lineup after the trade. Scoring begins the next full fantasy week.

Outcome ≠ process

Injuries and timing matter

A trade can be good process and poor outcome. The W/L column is deliberately labeled direct lineup utility, not a universal trade grade.

Legacy separately

Keeper / downstream value

Future flips, keeper value and voided 2023 draft picks can dramatically change the historical interpretation beyond same-season starter points.

Historical correction surfaced by the audit

The 2023 Stew/Kuhl blockbuster needs a more nuanced plaque.

That does not erase CeeDee’s elite season or the downstream trade tree—it means the old “obvious Stew win” label was too simplistic.
