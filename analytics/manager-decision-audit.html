Manager Decision Audit

Inspect documented draft decisions, verified trade sides and notable post-acquisition starters. This explorer presents the available evidence; it does not turn a highlights-only waiver sample into a waiver efficiency grade.

Audit limitations: Draft surplus is realized starter output compared with an archived positional baseline, not proof of a good or bad pick at draft time. Trade edges are direct realized outcomes, not causal counterfactuals. Waiver candidates are selectively archived highlights, not all bids or pickups. Historical lineup eligibility and FAAB opportunity costs remain unverified.

Source datasets: Draft outcomes · Verified trade sides · Acquisition highlights. The year filter applies to source records, not to an independently reconstructed full transaction history.

Exact evidence criteria and methodology

Draft evidence includes archived nonkeeper picks through 2025 with numeric surplus; the two most positive and two most negative measured picks are illustrated when available. Trade evidence requires a nonprovisional side with a confidence label beginning with “verified” and numeric direct edge; two positive and two negative sides are shown. Acquisitions show up to three archived post-addition starter-value highlights and do not imply rankings. Every displayed item identifies a year and relevant count or value. A missing category is “not sufficiently documented,” never zero.
