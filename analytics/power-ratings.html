Exactly how is the 100-point score calculated?

Eight bounded components add to a maximum of 100 points. The formula rewards both career accomplishments and sustained, recent performance. No leaderboard-place bonuses or hand-picked manager adjustments.

  • Championships 30
  • Verified runner-up finishes 5
  • Verified playoff appearances 10
  • Career regular-season record 20
  • Within-week all-play performance 15
  • Recent competitive form 5
  • Best completed season 10
  • Participation / longevity 5

Ring decay: each championship contributes an effective title value of 0.25 + 0.75 × 0.5^((2025 − championship year) / 3.5). Older rings never disappear, but have less influence on current power. Championship points = 30 × (1 − exp(−effective titles / 1.7)). Additional rings have diminishing returns; a fresh title improves the score more than one from long ago.

Recent form: historical win rate and all-play performance are weighted by recency (2.3-season half-life). 2026 games contribute at half weight because the season is incomplete. The best-season calculation uses completed seasons only. Career and within-season rates use small neutral priors to avoid overrating someone from a tiny sample.

Other dimensions: verified runner-up years use the same aging curve and a capped 5-point nonlinear component. Career record = 20 × smoothed win rate. All-play = 15 × smoothed within-week expected win rate. Recent form = 15 × a weighted blend of smoothed win and all-play rates. Peak = 10 × best smoothed completed-season record. Longevity = 5 × (1 − exp(−completed seasons / 5)). Component values and totals display to 0.1.

Incomplete historical playoff placements receive no inferred credit. All-play uses opponents competing in the same week, not raw cross-era points. 2026 is through Week 7; the rating will shift as additional games enter the canonical archive. These weights are editorial choices and can be versioned after league review.

All fourteen recovered managers are ranked. Former owners keep their separate historical identities; inactivity reduces the recent-form component without deleting their titles or record. Older experimental Legacy Score is a separate deprecated prototype.
