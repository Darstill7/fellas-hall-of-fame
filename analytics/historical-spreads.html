Method and limitations

Each team-season's points per game is compared with that season's league average and standard deviation of weekly team scores. The model then scales both teams' standardized scoring to the average of their two seasons' scoring deviations, takes the difference, and rounds to the nearest half-point. It does not use win–loss records to price the matchup. Postseason games are excluded.

Scoring settings and available games can differ by era. 2026 is ongoing. These exploratory numbers are not predictive probabilities or skill ratings.
