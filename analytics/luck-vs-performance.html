How to read this

Scoring strength: the percentage of other teams you would have beaten each week. Fifty percent is average. Schedule Luck: your actual wins minus what those weekly scores would have produced against a randomly selected opponent.

Upper right: strong scoring plus favorable opponents. Lower right: strong scoring despite tougher opponents. Upper left: weaker scoring with favorable opponents. Lower left: weaker scoring with tougher opponents.

This is a schedule-draw statistic, not a judgment about skill, injuries, roster decisions or championships. The 2016 league had eight teams; later seasons had ten. Comparisons use same-week opponent results and exclude incomplete pairing weeks in 2020–2021. Current-year data is provisional. Historical career records exclude 2026.
