A more useful look at what players contributed to each franchise, relative to other starters at the same position. All available seasons by default, with a season filter for deeper analysis.

Important: The rankings below show points above a position-specific starter benchmark and an optional matchup swing indicator, not finalized Wins Above Replacement. Multiple players can each be pivotal in the same win; their swing numbers are not additive team wins. The baseline is the lower-third average of league starters that week. We have not yet incorporated eligible free agents, benches, keeper costs, playoff player starts, or consistent position-specific opportunity. Don't treat these scores as official WAR or use them alone to declare trade winners.

How are these scores calculated?

We find the lowest-scoring third of players who actually started at each position in each week and use their average as a preliminary benchmark. A player's excess scoring is summed only for weeks started by that manager. Because these are observed low-end starters—not confirmed available alternatives—this is a research stage. Manager-by-manager attribution tracks the source owner identity; historical roster-eight ownership remains separate.

2020–2025 Weeks 1–14 plus 2026 Weeks 1–6; no playoff starter scores. *Matchup swing compares each actual lineup result against replacing one player at a time; it is not summed team WAR. Four roster-weeks could not be paired with a recorded opponent. Read methodology.
