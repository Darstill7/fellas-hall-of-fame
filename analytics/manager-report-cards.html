Report-card methodology and coverage

Drafting (25%): percentile rank of average measured post-draft starter-point surplus from nonkeeper picks, using the documented 2020–2025 draft engine. Requires at least 12 scored picks. This is outcome value, not proof of draft process.

Trading (25%): percentile of mean recorded direct-point edge per verified, nonprovisional trade side; four verified trade sides minimum. This measures realized direct outcomes, not counterfactual trade quality.

Winning (20%): smoothed career regular-season win percentage. All-play (15%): smoothed rate of outscoring same-week opponents, reducing schedule-luck distortion.

Waivers (10%) and Start/Sit (5%): Not graded. Pending comprehensive completed additions, eligible alternatives and weekly legal roster/bench coverage. The displayed provisional total reweights only graded categories, so it must not be compared as a final 100-point grade.

Minimum evidence: 12 draft picks / four verified trade sides. A category below its threshold displays N/A. All ratings are source-model estimates through the archived 2026 Week 7 snapshot.
