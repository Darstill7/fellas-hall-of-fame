How to read this score and its evidence

The completed 2025 season uses a descriptive research index with three components normalized to 0–100.

  1. 55% regular season: actual keeper starter points compared with the same week's median positional starters. This is not waiver replacement value.
  2. 30% nominal draft capital: keeper rounds, rewarding later-round retention. We have not verified every legal alternative at the keeper deadline.
  3. 15% championship contribution: keeper starter points only in actual championship-bracket matches. Consolation and placement games, including those after elimination, do not earn playoff credit. First-round byes earn no invented points.

Early releases, including Birch's Brian Robinson Jr. and Kuhl's Jared Goff, are verified events but receive no automatic deduction. Injury context, roster need, and actual replacement opportunity must be assessed before keeper-abandonment scoring.
