Method and data-source audit

Eligible draft picks are nonkeepers through the completed 2025 season with a numeric starter-point surplus, draft round and position. Each peer cohort matches year, round and position and excludes the selection being evaluated. The cohort must contain at least two other eligible picks. The aggregate is the unweighted mean of matched pick differences and is sensitive to outliers, sample size and missing selections.

Trade sides must be nonprovisional, explicitly labeled verified, and have numeric direct edge. Counting sides is not counting independent trade events; different sides from the same transaction may correlate.
