How we chose these moments

Weekly scoring stories use recorded head-to-head results from 2026 Weeks 1–6 only. Trade anniversaries come from the 51-event trade ledger, matched by the reported Sleeper transaction week and year. Trade presence is historical, not a judgment about who won the deal. Missing earlier matchup archives are not treated as empty seasons. Weekly scoring records do not claim all-time status across 2016–2025.
