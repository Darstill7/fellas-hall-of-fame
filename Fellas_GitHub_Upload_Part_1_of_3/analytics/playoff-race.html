What does the model believe?

Each manager’s scoring rate is blended 70% with the six-week observed average and 30% with the league average, which tempers extreme early results. Game-to-game volatility is estimated from recorded weekly scores and bounded between 19 and 38 points. Every future matchup is simulated, with wins and cumulative points used as an assumed standings tiebreaker. No player projections, injuries, or roster changes are incorporated.

Do not treat these as betting odds or definitive postseason predictions. Six playoff teams and first-round byes for seeds 1–2 are confirmed; tiebreaker and regular-season-end assumptions still require verification. The models will also need a refresh after Week 7 data is validated.
