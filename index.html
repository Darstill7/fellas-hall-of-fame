What does Legacy OVR mean?

Legacy OVR is a 65–99 career rating based on verified league success, including championships, playoff appearances, and regular-season results. It is not a win percentage.

Season OVR evaluates a single completed season. Report-card scores compare measured results across categories, but Drafting and Trading are provisional, and some skills remain unrated when evidence is incomplete.

These are different measures and should not be compared as if they use the same formula.
