# Fellas playoff format

- 10 teams in the league
- **Six** qualify for playoffs
- **Seeds 1–2** receive first-round byes
- Opening round: **3 vs. 6** and **4 vs. 5**
- Semifinals: Seeds 1 and 2 enter the bracket; exact reseeding rules not yet confirmed
