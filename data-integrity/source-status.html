How a new fantasy week becomes official

  1. Collect and stage Sleeper matchups, transactions and supporting receipts after games finish.
  2. Reconcile participants, scores, trade assets, standings, playoff boundaries and all downstream records.
  3. Approve once through the commissioner-reviewed canonical release path. Parallel candidate branches must not double-publish a week.
  4. Validate and publish on the exact integration commit, then compare the public Pages release against the expected source SHA and file contents.

Release snapshot recorded November 23, 2026: Week 10 has a separately staged/reviewed candidate but was not yet approved in main when this page update was authored. That is a dated project checkpoint, not a live approval signal. Consult weekly publication issue #275 for the latest review.

Research limitations that matter

Trade value and transaction history are distinct from verified fantasy scores. Historical comparisons may be incomplete because received assets, valid lineup replacement options, time-of-decision projections, keeper rights or FAAB evidence have not all been independently validated. A manager's missing trade +/- is not a zero, tie or loss.

For 2020–2021, championship-bracket playoffs began in Week 14; applying a universal Week 15 playoff cutoff would contaminate regular-season comparisons. Later eras and older ESPN aggregate formats need their own eligibility rules.
