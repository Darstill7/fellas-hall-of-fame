# Fellas original-provider ownership reconstruction — release gate

## Recoveries available
Sleeper transactions by week: 2020–2025 weeks 1–18; 2026 weeks 1–8. Raw normalized source retains completed/failed status, adds, drops, roster IDs, bid, and millisecond timestamps. Source-backed partial ownership events are generated by `scripts/build-faab-ownership-events.mjs` and crosschecked with `scripts/verify-faab-ownership-events.mjs`.

## Known limitations and fixes required
1. **Draft ownership baseline:** acquire verified opening roster player IDs per historical season. Without starting ownership, an unseen player is not a verified free agent.
2. **Trade ownership changes:** the recovered normalized transaction files currently retain type, IDs, added/dropped arrays, but not reliably full player-specific trade-side transfers; valuation-only `v9_trade_engine.json` uses many names without stable IDs. Import source trade-side player IDs and explicit recipient roster IDs before mutating trade ownership.
3. **Same-timestamp waiver processing:** equal millisecond timestamps are common; sorting by transaction ID is not a verified processing sequence. Preserve ambiguity and quarantine inferred ownership of affected players until independent evidence exists.
4. **Snapshot interpretation:** weekly scored rosters are not pre-claim roster inventories or free-agent lists; never treat a missing player in a snapshot as available.
5. **Preseason, playoffs and incomplete 2026:** preserve season-specific scoring, roster settings, trade deadlines, FAAB starting balances and special claim rules.
6. **Coverage auditing:** the provider connector's per-week records and counts are saved; crosscheck completeness and any source pagination limits before declaring the underlying complete universe certified.
7. **Player ownership intervals:** event-based intervals must stop at first verified drop, subsequent successful acquisition, trade, or ambiguity; no retroactive ownership after another manager acquires the player.
8. **No ratings from raw per-dollar points:** unavailable-player counterfactuals are not verified. Waiver report-card category remains N/A until baseline and tests permit certification.

## Explicit gate
A historical waiver alternative is **confirmed available** only when player identity, transaction-time ownership and waiver processing eligibility can be established through independent, temporally valid source records. Partial event ledgers are research data, not a confirmation of this gate.
