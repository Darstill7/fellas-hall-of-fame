# Schedule-fortune research — independent all-play recomputation (Nov 11, 2026)
**Status:** provisional derived-stat research, not official all-era historical certification.

## Method
For each full-scored-team regular-season week, compare each team's final score with **every other participating team**. One expected win is `(weekly all-play wins + 0.5 × all-play ties) / (scored teams − 1)`. A season's expected wins is the sum of those weekly expectations. **Schedule fortune = actual head-to-head wins + 0.5 × H2H ties − expected wins**, considering exactly those same qualifying weeks. This is a descriptive retrospective performance-vs-schedule measure, not proof of skill or independent luck.

2016 uses 8 participants and 7 possible weekly all-play opponents. The remaining ordinary weeks use 10 participants and 9 alternatives. **2020 and 2021 Week 14 are excluded for all managers, including their existing eight scored-team results, until bye-week/participant handling is certified. Thus those seasons below cover 13 scored weeks, not the complete league schedule.** Do not combine with official full-season winning records. The 2026 results cover nine weeks only.
