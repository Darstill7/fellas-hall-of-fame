# Archived evidence coverage audit

Repository has 188 data files, including 88 weekly Sleeper roster snapshots, yearly acquisition files for 2020–2026, yearly player-start files for 2020–2026, four recovered ESPN draft files, a 1,050-row Sleeper-era draft engine, trade source manifests, and verified playoff records.

## Safe to display

- Certified results: verified completed championship and playoff records, canonical WLT, and all-play.
- Provisional estimates: documented nonkeeper draft pick surplus (minimum 12 picks) and verified nonprovisional trade-side direct edge (minimum four sides). Neither is a certified decision-quality rating.
- Waivers: recorded completed acquisitions and subsequent-start highlights. The selected candidate list is NOT a full denominator; do not assign a category skill score.
- Lineups: weekly starters and recovered rosters support descriptive and hindsight research, not full ex-ante choice quality or a final skill score. Missing prior-lock availability and historical slot certainty remain blockers.
- ESPN era 2016–2019: documented season results and reconstructed drafts, but no equivalent complete action and roster-alternative history. Do not substitute missing data with zeros.
- 2026: still ongoing; some source feeds have different latest completed weeks.

## Implementation policy

Keep Legacy OVR, Season OVR, and provisional report-card grades as three different concepts. Continue to use the existing report-card draft/trade thresholds. Display verified results, provisional outcome estimates, and ungraded skills explicitly. Never promote selected waiver highlights, hindsight lineup optimizations, or observed direct trade edges into certified decision-skill grades.
