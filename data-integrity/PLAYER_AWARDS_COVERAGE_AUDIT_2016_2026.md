# Player-Level Awards Coverage Audit · 27 October 2026

## Scope and result
The Hall of Fame covers **one league** across ESPN (2016–2019) and Sleeper (2020–2026 Week 7). The score-based historical awards are supported by the combined week-level archive, but those scores alone do **not** establish player MVP, waiver success, draft steal or trade outcome rankings.

## Confirmed available
- **Team-level 2016–2025:** scoring crowns, wins, high/low weeks, margin records, titles and original ESPN championship finalists from the original scorecards or champion ledger.
- **2026:** seven weeks available, provisional only.
- **Sleeper 2020–2026:** existing Starter Value MVP and Waiver Wire Gem candidates, designated experimental in the annual awards source, not official league-wide all-time honors.
- **2016–2019:** no complete player starts, roster movement, waiver or draft history currently established as an equivalent source to the Sleeper award analysis.

## Publication rules
1. Manager trophies are credited across the entire documented league.
2. Player and waiver awards remain in a distinct section with the years and the **experimental** status plainly displayed.
3. The source contains legacy awards that may involve **postseason** points. Those awards must not be counted in a regular-season scoring record. The manager trophy widget restricts scoreboard honors to audited regular-season labels.
4. Do not declare all-time player MVP, greatest waiver move or greatest trade across 2016–2025 until all eligible historical transaction and starting-lineup data are recovered and evaluated with consistent methodology.
5. The 2020 and 2021 Week 14 absences were playoff byes, not missing matchups.
6. The website's ESPN/Sleeper historical pages are linked by common owner identities; 'Tom' means Zach Prescott (Thommy), while Tom Schermerhorn remains a separate former owner.
