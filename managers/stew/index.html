The Franchise Story

A founding manager since 2016, Drew Sylvester has been a constant opponent across every chapter of the league. The 2022 team went 11–3 and led in both points and potential points, showing how formidable a roster the constant dealing can produce. His archive includes major acquisitions involving Justin Jefferson, Ja’Marr Chase and Puka Nacua; it also includes trades that invite second-guessing. The fascinating part of Stew’s legacy is the gap between roster construction and the trophy case. He has assembled elite regular-season teams without yet turning that volume of moves into a documented championship. The archived 2016–2026 Week 7 regular-season record is 66–76 over 142 games. Across the complete recovered archive, Stew has faced Stilly 20 times, with a 10–10 regular-season series. These are continuous Fellas results across both platforms; trade and player-level research is better documented in the Sleeper years.
