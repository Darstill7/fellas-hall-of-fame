The Franchise Story

Thommy is one of the four original 2016 managers. His three straight ESPN championships in 2016, 2017 and 2018 are the league’s first verified dynasty, followed by the 2024 championship. The original three-peat from 2016 through 2018 turned one successful season into a dynasty; the 2024 title proved that era was not the end of the story. Individual transaction metrics measure different outcomes from the four documented championship finishes and do not provide a complete evaluation of roster-building. Tee Higgins and Christian McCaffrey headline his recorded player contributions, but the defining figure is the manager behind four trophies. When the Fellas debate greatness, Tom starts with the simplest statistic: championships. The archived regular-season record is 67–74–1 over 142 games (through the latest verified archive). Across the complete recovered archive, Thommy has faced Stew 16 times, with a 9–7 regular-season series. These are continuous Fellas results across both platforms; trade and player-level research is better documented in the Sleeper years.
